🔥 The Roast: Week 5

Two managers tied the median to the hundredth, Brad lost by half a point with the answer on his bench, the commish is on his third kicker since Monday night, and Brian is somehow in first. Tap if you can take it.

⏰ New roasts drop every Wednesday at 6 PM ET, right after waivers and before Thursday night.

Wow, that just happened. What's next? Week 4 gave us a 214.75 beatdown, two teams that scored exactly the median, a commissioner who left the win sitting on his bench, and Coastal Elites going 2-0. Brian Mulvihill is in first place and nobody saw him get there. Week 5 kicks off Thursday, and nobody is safe, least of all the Dictator.

1
Ryan Nussdorfer · THE Dictator · 4-4

The Dictator opened the week trying to bribe the league cop with a little "tip the scale" joke, then got outscored by Drew. A dictator who can't even run a corruption scandal correctly. Emanuel Wilson put up 28.0 on his bench, and Ryan's best possible lineup was 183.5 against Drew's 174.25. The win was sitting right there on the pine and the commissioner looked straight past it. Then he went into Monday night needing a comeback and fell 29.35 short, like a guy sprinting after the bus, slapping the back of it, and watching it pull away. Look-back: projected 151.3, scored 144.9, and my call of a 20-point win was flat wrong. Wednesday he claimed Ryan Fitzgerald at 3:12 AM and cut him for Ka'imi Fairbairn at 8:42 AM. Five and a half hours. That's not a roster move, that's a Tinder match. It's his third kicker since Monday Night Football, and Fairbairn is the kicker Taylor cut in Week 3. Ryan plays Taylor this week. You're dumpster-diving in your opponent's trash, Commish. And he's STILL #1 in the power rankings with a league-high 645.4 points, all from behind a middle-school stache that looks like it's waiting on its learner's permit.

This week
vs Taylor Alvarado (2-6). Projected 145.0 to 135.7. Tyler Shough (15.5) sits for Jordan Love (16.8) at superflex, and Braelon Allen (15.1) is on the bench behind Jeremiyah Love (12.6). Emanuel Wilson flashbacks loading.

🔮 Prediction: Ryan wins by 10, Fairbairn shanks an extra point, and the Commish is on kicker number four before Thursday night.

2
Bradley Kochheiser · The Implication · 3-4-1

The league sweetheart scored exactly 147.9, the median to the hundredth, and tied it. Showed up looking great, did nothing, left early. Parker Washington started and scored 2.0. Devaughn Vele sat on the bench with 17.4. Swap those two and Brad beats Cardillo 163.3 to 162.8. HALF. A. POINT. He lost to Nick Cardillo by a rounding error he personally chose. His Saints defense scored NEGATIVE 5.9, which means the D/ST was technically playing for Cardillo. Look-back: Brad beat his projection by 3.4 and still lost, and I picked him to win, so we both got played. Wednesday he finally cut the Saints D for Cincinnati, dumped Boswell (3.0) for Jake Bates, and claimed Jacoby Brissett to cover Mahomes' bye. A genuinely productive morning. And then he set his lineup and started Parker Washington again.

This week
vs Jacob Maddox. Projected 141.3 to 144.4. Mahomes is on bye, so Brissett (18.3) starts at superflex, and yes, Parker Washington (14.2) starts over Vele (9.8) one more time.

🔮 Prediction: Brad loses by less than Parker Washington scores, goes quiet, and the chat sends a search party.

That's spending forty hours on a Minecraft castle and leaving a lava pit through the throne room. Still zero waiver moves all season. His one trade: DK Metcalf for Malik Willis. This week Willis is on the bench projected 15.6 while Stroud starts at 15.9. That's the entire return on a Pro Bowl receiver: a 0.3-point coin flip he doesn't even get to use. Look-back: projected 151.8, scored 147.9, and my lean on Kasik hit, because Gibbs did not go nuclear. He went 17.7.

This week
vs Brad Kochheiser. Projected 144.4 to 141.3. Bucky Irving is questionable at flex, and Gibbs (25.8) has to carry the whole thing again.

And we'll hunt him for it. Because he can take it. Because he's not our hero. He's a silent guardian, a watchful protector of first place... a Dark Knight.
