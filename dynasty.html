  6. 6MagicSkolBussss Mike Hemstreet · 5-1 · ▼1

  10. 10Coastal Elites Nick Cardillo · 1-5 · –

How it's calculated
  • Overall = 50% Win-Now + 50% Dynasty, scored against the other teams.
  • Win-Now = 50% results (record incl. the median game, points for, all-play) + 50% best possible lineup from Sleeper projections (rest of the regular season and this week), in our scoring.
  • Dynasty = total trade value of every rostered player plus 2027–2028 rookie picks, from FantasyCalc (superflex, 10 teams, PPR=1).
  • Status: Contender = top 4 in Win-Now; Rebuild = bottom 3 in Win-Now and outside the playoff line; everyone else Middle.

🔥 The Roast: Week 4

Ten managers, zero mercy. 112 points rotting on a bench, a commish who can't pick his own QB, and an 0-6 team hoarding draft picks like canned food. Tap if you can take it.

⏰ New roasts drop every Wednesday at 6 PM ET, right after waivers and before Thursday night.

Wow, that just happened. What's next? Weeks 1–3 are in the books, Week 4 is here, and nobody is safe, least of all the commissioner.

1
Ryan Nussdorfer · Mr McGibblets · 4-2

THE Dictator. Commissioner, rule-maker, self-appointed king, and somehow the only man in America who can't figure out which of his own quarterbacks to start. Week 1 he started Bo Nix for 1.75 fucking points, benched Jordan Love's 21.85, and lost to a rookie GM who'd been in the league for about fifteen minutes. Week 3 he benched Bo Nix and Bo Nix dropped 25.5. You're losing coin flips to the coin, Ryan, and you wrote the rules for the coin. He traded for Dylan Sampson and cut him before he played a single snap, the most expensive way anyone has ever said "never mind." Then he ran Michael Mayer, Terrance Ferguson and Juwan Johnson through his tight end spot like a divorced guy on a dating app. And the worst part? He still leads the league with 500.5 points. Dumb as hell and rewarded for it. That's not a commissioner, that's a nepo baby who hired himself.

This week
vs Drew Hartman (0-6). Projected 151.5 to 131.0, with Chase, Olave, Bowers, and two QBs he's going to fuck up the order of anyway.

🔮 Prediction: Ryan wins by 20 and posts about it in the group chat like he personally liberated France.

2
Bradley Kochheiser · The Implication · 3-3

Brad has Josh Allen AND Patrick Mahomes and he's 3-3. You own the two best quarterbacks on Earth and you're .500. That's a Michelin-star chef working the fryer at a Golden Corral. He's eaten a league-worst 560.5 points against, and in Week 1 his 153.1 got steamrolled by Taylor's 232.5, the biggest score of the year. That's not bad luck, that's the fantasy gods keying your car. Week 3 he started Jadarian Price for 0.2 points. ZERO POINT FUCKING TWO. Meanwhile Kenyon Sadiq dropped 26.0 on his bench. And this week Price is questionable and STILL starting over Kamara. The team's called The Implication, and the implication is that Brad will keep starting Jadarian Price until one of them files a restraining order.

This week
vs Nick Cardillo. Projected 142.3 to 118.3. Price (8.1, questionable) starts over Kamara (10.0) AGAIN, and Breece Hall is doubtful.

🔮 Prediction: Brad wins, because even Jadarian Price can't lose to Cardillo's RB room. Probably.

FIVE AND ONE with ZERO waiver moves. The man won't touch the waiver wire like it's a public toilet seat. His Sleeper name is RyansHero. A grown-ass adult named himself after the commissioner like he got him at Build-A-Bear. He started Colston Loveland for a big fat 0.0 in Week 1 and won anyway, because he played Drew. Malik Nabers gave him 1.1 in Week 2 and he won anyway, because Taylor shit the bed with 88. Second-softest schedule in the league (358.3 against). You're not a contender, you're a guy getting dealt pocket aces by a dealer with cataracts. And he traded DK Metcalf for Malik Willis. Jacob looked at a Pro Bowl wide receiver and said "nah, I'd rather have a clipboard."

This week
vs Nick Kasik. Projected 151.9 to 155.8, a 4-point underdog. Jahmyr Gibbs (24.9 projected) has to carry this fraud again.

🔮 Prediction: Projections lean Kasik, but if Gibbs goes nuclear Jacob goes 7-1 and still doesn't make a single fucking waiver claim.

That's ordering a ribeye and eating the parsley. He paid a 2027 2nd for Terry McLaurin, and McLaurin gave him 3.4 points in Week 1. That's paying for a lap dance and getting a firm handshake. Now Caleb Williams is doubtful, so Mr. SukmyDitkasbro is starting Deshaun Watson off waivers at superflex. In 2026. When your Plan B was last relevant during Covid, your season is held together with duct tape and denial. His only loss was to Ryan by 7.4, and Ryan will bring it up at every draft until 2040.
