  1. 1Mr McGibblets Ryan Nussdorfer · 4-2 · ▲1

    Most points in the league (500.5), capped by a week-high 202.3 in Week 3, plus the No. 1 dynasty roster built on Ja'Marr Chase, Brock Bowers and rookie Jeremiyah Love. The commish dealt Nicholas Singleton and Denzel Boston to Drew for Marvin Harrison Jr. and Dylan Sampson, then cut Sampson before Week 1. The ROS projection is only 6th, so the throne isn't locked yet.

  2. 2The Implication Bradley Kochheiser · 3-3 · ▼1

    Josh Allen (39.0, 41.8) and Patrick Mahomes in the superflex give this roster the No. 2 dynasty value (KTC has it No. 1). Taking a league-worst 560.5 points against is the only reason it's 3-3 with a 16-11 all-play. Alvin Kamara came off waivers for Khalil Shakir in Week 3.

  3. 3HulkSmash Jacob Maddox · 5-1 · –

    5-1, scoring went up every week (143 → 153 → 172), and Jahmyr Gibbs already has a 43.4 and a 35.6. The model has Win-Now only 6th because rest-of-season projections rank 7th and the 358.3 points against has been kind. The DK Metcalf-for-Malik Willis swap with Mike says Jacob is thinking long-term, and the 24.8 value-weighted age backs that up.

  4. 4Wubba Lubba Dub Ws Brian Mulvihill · 5-1 · –

    Jaxon Smith-Njigba put up 45.0 in Week 2, the best single game in the league so far, and 37.5 in Week 3. Brian is 5-1 after two Week 1 deals with Drew (Pollard and Shaheed for Zavion Thomas; a 2027 2nd for Terry McLaurin). Caleb Williams is listed doubtful, so off-the-wire Deshaun Watson is the Week 4 superflex. Watch that.

  5. 5BaldBy22 Nicholas Kasik · 3-3 · ▲1

    That 85.55 in Week 2 is the lowest score of the season and the reason Nick is 3-3 with only the 8th-most points. But the roster projects 2nd-best the rest of the way (151.4/wk) behind Bijan Robinson (39.8 in Week 3). He also won the Aaron Jones claim in Week 2 over Ryan and Brad.

  6. 6MagicSkolBussss Mike Hemstreet · 5-1 · ▼1

    The rookie GM is 5-1 and No. 1 in Win-Now, with the best rest-of-season projection (152.9/wk) behind Derrick Henry, Christian McCaffrey and Lamar Jackson. The bill comes later: the oldest roster in the league (29.2 value-weighted age), Dynasty No. 9, and the most waiver churn of anyone (14 adds), with A.J. Brown and Tank Dell on IR.

  7. 7Gronk Nation Kyle Llano · 2-4 · –

    2-4 despite the 5th-most points, because only Brad has given up more (535.55). Kyle has also left the most on the bench: D'Andre Swift's 34.4 in Week 1 and Sam Darnold's 33.45 in Week 3 both sat. The roster projects 3rd-best rest-of-season, so the record should come back up even with De'Von Achane on IR.

  8. 8EastLansingNatureBoy Taylor Alvarado · 2-4 · –

    Week 1 was the season's best score (232.5, with Ashton Jeanty, Christian Watson and Justin Jefferson all over 30). Week 2 came in at 88.2. Taylor paid Fernando Mendoza plus a 2027 2nd for Jaxson Dart, and Dart is now on IR with Marcus Mariota filling the superflex. Brock Purdy (31.85, 34.75) has been doing the heavy lifting.

  9. 9What Parents? Drew Hartman · 0-6 · –

    0-6 with a 4-23 all-play, but Drew is the league's busiest trader: four Week 1 deals left him with eight 2027/28 picks, including three 2027 2nds. That's enough for Dynasty No. 5 (No. 4 on KTC) around Puka Nacua, James Cook and Jayden Daniels. This week he dropped Cam Ward for Geno Smith, so he's still shuffling the QB room.

  10. 10Coastal Elites Nick Cardillo · 1-5 · –

    The fewest points (322.5), last in Win-Now, and an injury report with Travis Etienne out, Jonathon Brooks on IR and Zach Charbonnet on PUP. FantasyCalc ranks the roster last in dynasty too, but KTC likes the young core (Drake Maye, Tetairoa McMillan, Luther Burden, taxi rookie Carnell Tate) enough to put it 5th. So call it a rebuild with upside.

How it's calculated
  • Overall = 50% Win-Now + 50% Dynasty, scored against the other teams.
  • Win-Now = 50% results (record incl. the median game, points for, all-play) + 50% best possible lineup from Sleeper projections (rest of the regular season and this week), in our scoring.
  • Dynasty = total trade value of every rostered player plus 2027–2028 rookie picks, from FantasyCalc (superflex, 10 teams, PPR=1).
  • Status: Contender = top 4 in Win-Now; Rebuild = bottom 3 in Win-Now and outside the playoff line; everyone else Middle.
