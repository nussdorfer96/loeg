🔥 The Roast: Week 7

Maddox threatened to throw every game and is somehow still 5-9 against the Commish, Brad kept donating, Mike ran a witness relocation program for quarterbacks, and Taylor hired defense number nine. Tap if you can take it.

⏰ New roasts drop every Wednesday at 6 PM ET, right after waivers and before Thursday night.

Wow, that just happened. What's next? Week 6 gave us a 68-point blowout, a commissioner's emergency press conference, two trades that went exactly one direction, and a running back who got zero touches in somebody's starting lineup. Week 7 kicks off Thursday. Nobody is safe.

1
Jacob Maddox · TheBelt-Less · 9-2-1

First place. 9-2-1. And this man's big move of the week was threatening to quit the league and throw every game if the Commish put Shedeur Sanders back on his roster. Throw every game! That's a hostage negotiation where the hostage is your own team! Then he left Tre Tucker and Colston Loveland on his bench for 45.7 points and STILL beat Cardillo by 25, so even with 45 points on the pine, Maddox can't lose to Cardillo. Best team in the league, everybody's scared of him, the belt is practically his. And against the Commish? Five and nine. FIVE. AND. NINE. You can run the whole league, Jake. You just can't run past Ryan.

This week
vs Mike Hemstreet, 9-2-1 against 8-4, first place against third-best record. Maddox threatened to quit the league if Shedeur came back, which is the only threat Mike's quarterback room has ever survived.

🔮 Prediction: Maddox wins by 30 and announces he would have won by 60 if he were trying.

2
Ryan Nussdorfer · THE Dictator · 5-7

The Commish called an emergency press conference, sat down with one ankle up on his knee like a guy selling a timeshare from a lawn chair, and told the league nothing needs to change. Nothing needs to change. Blake Corum sat on his bench for the second straight week while Emanuel Wilson started for 5.2. "The legs feed the wolf," he said. Ryan, the wolf has lost three in a row. He says he's second in points and sixth in luck, which is what a man says when the scoreboard keeps disagreeing with him. And this week he picked Stefon Diggs back up, seven days after cutting him. Even the guy in charge can't stand behind his own ruling. Chin up.

This week
vs Nick Kasik, three straight losses into a week after an emergency presser that promised nothing would change. Good news: nothing did.

🔮 Prediction: Ryan loses by 8, holds another press conference, and still doesn't change anything.

3
Bradley Kochheiser · The Implication · 5-6-1

Brad has stopped losing games and started donating them. Four straight head-to-head losses, and he's had more points scored against him than anybody in the league, by over a hundred. Every week some guy walks in, takes a win off the table, and Brad asks if he wants a receipt. He benched Xavier Worthy for Parker Washington, and his kicker Nick Folk gave him one point. ONE. So Brad cut Folk, the only guy on that roster who was ever held accountable. At this point Brad should file the whole season as a charitable deduction. The IRS would take one look and approve it.

This week
vs Brian Mulvihill: four straight losses against four straight wins. Brad isn't facing a heater this week, he's the fuel for it.

🔮 Prediction: Brad loses by 30 and gets a thank-you card from Brian.

4
Brian Mulvihill · Wubba Lubba Dub Ws · 9-3

Brian won by 68.15, four straight, 9-3, and then on Monday afternoon, with every one of his starters already done for the week, he picked up Rachaad White. Couldn't start him. Didn't need him. White put up 15.7 on Monday night, on the bench, where Brian could sit in the Batcave and watch. Billionaire move, buying a running back to keep in the trophy room. Batman's got a utility belt full of gadgets he never uses, and Brian's got a bench full of points he never needed. You either die a hero or live long enough to become the villain. Brian's 9-3. He picked villain.

This week
vs Brad Kochheiser, 9-3 with four straight wins against a guy on four straight losses. Gotham's billionaire finally found a charity that accepts W's.

🔮 Prediction: Brian wins by 35 and nobody hears him do it.

5
Kyle Llano · Gronk Nation · 5-7

Kyle beat the Commish while leaving Germie Bernard on his bench for 25.7, which says less about Kyle and more about the Commish. But what is the deal with Kyle and Michigan? He hates Michigan. Can't stand them. So why do I get the feeling he watched every snap Saturday? Michigan beat Penn State 21-18 after benching Bryce Underwood at halftime for a guy named Tommy Carr. Nobody knows Tommy Carr! Tommy Carr's parents had to check the roster! Michigan doesn't know Kyle exists, and Kyle's still got them living in his head rent-free, no lease, with a parking spot and a key to the mailbox.

This week
vs Drew Hartman, 5-7 against a 6-6 guy on a three-game win streak. Kyle's focus is fully locked in, right up until Michigan kicks off Saturday.

🔮 Prediction: Kyle wins by 12 and spends Saturday watching Michigan anyway.

6
Drew Hartman · What Parents? · 6-6

Drew's out here calling himself the Super Bowl halftime show. JAY-Z! Brother, you traded James Cook and Chuba Hubbard to Mike, and they put up 48.4 for Mike that same week, while Derrick Henry gave you 4.0 and DJ Moore gave you nothing! You had 99 problems and you traded two running backs for problem number 100! And he STILL won by 46.6, three straight, because Kirk Cousins and Isaiah Likely bailed him out. Call it a halftime show if you want. It was lip-syncing, and the crowd cheered anyway because they'd already paid for the tickets. But I love the energy, man. Wrong decisions, all the confidence in the world.

This week
vs Kyle Llano, 6-6 and three straight wins after a trade week that sent his points to Mike. Drew is the only GM in America whose trades help him win by leaving.

🔮 Prediction: Drew loses by 10 and offers somebody a trade before the Sunday night game ends.

7
Mike Hemstreet · MagicSkolBussss · 8-4

Mike went through quarterbacks this week like a guy at a gas station working through scratch-offs. He added Jalon Daniels, dropped him, added him again, dropped him again, then landed on Tyler Huntley. Started Jameis Winston for 6.0, and the Giants pulled Jameis for J.J. McCarthy. So what does Mike do? Cuts Jameis, cuts Huntley, and picks up McCarthy, the guy who took Jameis's job. He's not building a QB room, he's running a witness relocation program. And everybody nods along, because watch out, he's a cop. Oh, and he started A.J. Brown for 4.7 with DeVonta Smith on his bench for 20.5. Still won. Must be nice having friends on the force.

This week
vs Jacob Maddox, 8-4 against first place. Mike added and dropped the same quarterback twice in four minutes, so the league leader is just the second-scariest thing in his transaction log.

🔮 Prediction: Mike loses by 25 and adds and drops three quarterbacks before Thursday kickoff.

8
Nicholas Kasik · BaldBy22 · 5-7

Kasik is the baby of this league, and he managed like it: 90.05 points, a 68-point loss, and MarShawn Lloyd started for 1.0 while J.K. Dobbins sat on the bench with 13.6. The baby found the remote and put it in his mouth. Everybody else in this league has been screwing up lineups for a decade; Kasik's still on his learner's permit, and the lawyer in him wants you to know it's technically not his fault. Take notes, young buck. And somebody keep an eye on the kid, because Maddox already noticed him getting awfully close with the other Nick, the big-city lawyer and the working man, and called it a love story.

This week
vs Ryan Nussdorfer, the Commish, who's on a three-game skid. He just sent Herbert and his 2027 and 2028 firsts to the other Nick for Trevor Lawrence. That's not a trade, that's a dowry.

🔮 Prediction: Kasik wins by 8 and bills the Commish for the press conference.

9
Taylor Alvarado · EastLansingNatureBoy · 4-8

Taylor started Cam Skattebo, and the Giants gave Cam Skattebo zero touches. Zero! Six snaps, no ball. He was in Taylor's lineup like a fire extinguisher: technically there, only in case of emergency. Meanwhile Omar Cooper put up 24.8 and Kyle Pitts 23.0, on the bench. And this morning Taylor fired the Colts defense and hired the Titans. That's defense number nine. I predicted number nine by Friday. It showed up five days late, which for Taylor counts as early. Taylor goes through defenses like a Trump cabinet: loyalty for a week, then a firing by social media post, then a new guy who's somehow worse.

This week
vs Nick Cardillo, 4-8 against 3-9. Taylor promised 8 defenses in 4 weeks, delivered in 6, and now faces the one opponent who makes any of them look like a good start.

🔮 Prediction: Taylor wins by 25, and defense number ten arrives by Wednesday.

10
Nick Cardillo · Coastal Elites · 3-9

Cardillo got 31.4 out of Trevor Lawrence and still lost by 25, because his tight end and kicker combined for 2.9 points. Brandon Aubrey, the best leg in football, kicked for one point. One! That's the Cardillo move: walk up, say one thing, leave. That's also exactly what happens when Cardillo sees a pretty girl. He freezes up like he's trying to remember his own name, mumbles something about UMass, and backs away like he owes her money. 3-9, and he still can't get a word out to a pretty girl. Thank God there's another Nick in this league. That's why friends call him Gay Nick.

This week
vs Taylor Alvarado, 3-9 against 4-8. Cardillo just landed Herbert and two Kasik firsts for Trevor Lawrence, so he finally got a commitment from somebody.

🔮 Prediction: Cardillo loses by 30, and somehow the other Nick takes it harder.
