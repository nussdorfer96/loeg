🔥 The Roast: Week 6

Brad took the biggest beating of the season, Maddox broke an Unbreakable Vow and the Commish re-bound it at 4:51 this afternoon, Taylor kept his eight-defense promise, and overtime murdered the median. Tap if you can take it.

⏰ New roasts drop every Wednesday at 6 PM ET, right after waivers and before Thursday night.

Wow, that just happened. What's next? Week 5 gave us a 70.7-point blowout, the biggest of the season, three overtime games that moved the median, a commissioner who spent his birthday weekend re-adding a third-string quarterback to somebody else's roster, and a Drew Hartman who now says "watch out" with a straight face. Week 6 kicks off Thursday. Nobody is safe.

1
Ryan Nussdorfer · THE Dictator · 5-5

I tell ya, the Commish gets no respect. Saturday was his birthday, the chat crowned him Supreme Leader, and at 9:37 that night the Supreme Leader was force-feeding Shedeur Sanders back onto Maddox's roster, with Brian Lewerke thrown in for good measure. Then he lost to Taylor by 4.75 with Braelon Allen (20.6), Blake Corum (16.2) and Emanuel Wilson (13.2) on his bench while Jeremiyah Love started and scored 2.2. Braelon, you let the whole team down. Blake, you let the whole team down. Emanuel, you let the whole team down. Except they didn't, Commish. You did. This morning he cut Deebo Samuel and Stefon Diggs. Finally, a roster move on his own team.

This week
vs Kyle Llano. Projected 151.6 to 145.6. Braelon Allen (11.4) is on the bench again, behind Jeremiyah Love (10.1).

🔮 Prediction: Ryan wins by 6, and Braelon Allen scores 15 on the bench, because of course he does.

2
Bradley Kochheiser · The Implication · 4-5-1

Sunday, 6:50 PM, games still going, Brad tells the chat he fears he's headed for his second press conference of the season. Brother, that's not fear, that's a man reading the scoreboard. Maddox beat him 218.2 to 147.5, a 70.7-point beating and the biggest blowout of the season, and Monday the Commish made it official: questions due, video by Thursday. Brad has now given up 941.5 points in five weeks. Maddox put it best: tough look when you're getting 180+ a week scored against you. Brad's own scouting report: 1-4 straight up, 3-1-1 against the median. Listen. When the only thing in your life that's nice to you is a math formula, you need new friends. Parker Washington even scored 32.9 for him. Didn't matter. Nothing matters.

This week
vs Mike Hemstreet. Projected 144.0 to 133.1. Mahomes is back next to Josh Allen, and the new kicker (Nick Folk, off waivers) and new defense (Chiefs, added at 6:01 this morning) cover the Detroit and Cincinnati byes.

🔮 Prediction: Brad wins by 11 and still gives up 130, which for him counts as a rest day.

3
Jacob Maddox · TheBelt-Less · 7-2-1

Maddox scored 218.2, the high score of the week, and beat Brad by 70.7, a few days after his own press conference got rave reviews. A great week for a team now called TheBelt-Less. But you can't talk about Maddox's week without Shedeur Sanders. He cut him Friday, Saturday, Sunday, and twice on Monday. Monday afternoon he offered Gibbs, Nabers, Garrett Wilson, Bucky Irving, Matthew Golden and the Vikings defense to anyone who'd take the curse off him. The Commish said he hoped Maddox had learned his lesson, the cut a minute later stuck, and for two days Maddox was a free man. Then today at 4:51 PM, the Commish put Shedeur back for the fifth time. Turns out it was an Unbreakable Vow. In Harry Potter, you break one and you die. In this league, you break one and it just keeps showing up on your roster. And I think that's worse.

This week
vs Nick Cardillo. Projected 124.0 to 111.0. Maddox is starting the Vikings defense, the same Vikings D he offered up in the Shedeur ransom, and Minnesota is on bye. That's a zero in the lineup until he fixes it. Bucky Irving is questionable, and Shedeur is on the bench, where the vow can keep an eye on him.

🔮 Prediction: Maddox wins anyway, cuts Shedeur by Friday, and Shedeur is back by Saturday.

4
Brian Mulvihill · Wubba Lubba Dub Ws · 7-3

Brian beat Kyle 137.75 to 129.6 and sits second at 7-3, which is a lot of winning for a man whose kicker, Cairo Santos, scored zero. Not negative. Zero. Now I'm no kicking expert, but I believe the idea is you kick it. Jaylen Warren did the actual work with 39.8, and Brian said nothing about it, because Brian never says anything about anything. Somewhere in New Mexico tonight there's a silhouette on a rooftop, a cape moving in the wind, and a masked figure quietly sitting second in the standings. He rises. He just doesn't text.

This week
vs Nick Kasik. Projected 142.8 to 151.6. Bryce Young starts with Deshaun Watson at superflex, and Caleb Williams (doubtful) stays on the bench.

🔮 Prediction: Brian loses by 8 and nobody hears about it until Tuesday.

5
Nicholas Kasik · BaldBy22 · 5-5

Kasik lost to Mike by 4.0. What is the deal with lawyers? They tell you to read everything before you sign, and then Kasik signs off on Justin Herbert at superflex (6.95) with Daniel Jones on his bench (13.75). That swap alone wins it. The Seahawks defense put up 11.3 on the bench while the Jaguars started for 5.0, and Jameson Williams scored 18.7 back there too. The evidence was all in the file, counselor. You just never opened the file. And losing to the cop? Bad look for the league's lawyer. This morning he dropped AJ Barner for MarShawn Lloyd and is starting him at flex, the kind of motion you file after the verdict's already in.

This week
vs Brian Mulvihill. Projected 151.6 to 142.8. Zay Flowers and Nico Collins are both questionable starters, and Daniel Jones finally starts at QB next to Herbert.

🔮 Prediction: Kasik wins by 9 and bills Brian for the hours.

6
Mike Hemstreet · MagicSkolBussss · 6-4

Mike had the most stressful win in America. Sunday night he's in Ravens-Falcons overtime trying to survive, and he makes it, thanks to what he calls "Tua turntheballover," and beats Kasik by 4. Then Monday night, with no players in the game, he sits and watches Bills-Rams go to overtime, and a blocked kick knocks him out of the median by under a point. You know what that is? That's getting pulled over AFTER you're already home. Meanwhile T.J. Hockenson, benched two weeks ago for a 26.9, finally got the start and scored 1.7. Mike and Hockenson break up and get back together more than anybody I know, and Mike gets burned every time. Sunday morning he also added Jameis Winston, dropped him eight minutes later for Tyreek Hill, a man with no team, and this morning burned a waiver claim to get Jameis back. Watch out, he's a cop. He arrests the same guy twice.

This week
vs Brad Kochheiser. Projected 133.1 to 144.0. Lamar Jackson (questionable, 21.4 projected) sits for Jameis Winston (13.1), Kelce is back at tight end with Hockenson on bye, and Mike Evans and DeVonta Smith are questionable starters.

🔮 Prediction: Mike loses by 7, and Lamar's points on the bench get entered into evidence.

7
Kyle Llano · Gronk Nation · 4-6

Kyle posted 129.6, the lowest score of the week, lost to Brian by 8.15, and in the middle of it, Sunday at 6:27 PM, stopped to ask the Commish why a fumble that isn't even lost costs a point. Fumble not lost. Think about that phrase. You dropped it, everybody saw you drop it, and you want credit because somebody on your team picked it up. The math: one point per fumble, an 8.15-point loss, so the league would have to reverse nine fumbles to flip it. The Commish's official ruling: don't put the ball on the turf. And Saturday there wasn't even Michigan football to hate, because Michigan was on bye. Even on a bye week, Michigan doesn't pay rent.

This week
vs Ryan Nussdorfer. Projected 145.6 to 151.6. Goff and St. Brown are on the Lions' bye, so Stafford and Sam Darnold run the quarterback spots.

🔮 Prediction: Kyle loses by 6 and files a formal complaint about the fumble rule. Denied.

8
Drew Hartman · What Parents? · 4-6

Sunday at 8:01 PM, mid-beatdown, Drew tells the chat he'd been trying to trade Jayden Daniels and Sam LaPorta to Cardillo this week. "Hah silly me." Daniels and LaPorta then put 53.55 on Cardillo, and Drew won 212.55 to 145.45, the second-best score of the week. You tried to sell the guy the gun, and then you shot him with it! Monday the Commish asked if Drewbie can be good all of a sudden, and Drew fired back: mid-season adjustments, team got healthy, watch out. With the smirk emoji. Two straight 2-0 weeks. Saturday, Pat Fitzgerald flipped the bird at Illinois' sideline in a one-point overtime loss. Sunday, the fieriest man in East Lansing went out and actually won. That's a coach you follow into war.

This week
vs Taylor Alvarado. Projected 138.8 to 136.6. Jayden Daniels, Puka Nacua and Jalen Coker are all questionable in the starting lineup, with Geno Smith (13.6) waiting on the bench.

🔮 Prediction: Drew wins by 3 and posts "watch out" again, unprompted.

9
Taylor Alvarado · EastLansingNatureBoy · 4-6

Last Wednesday night, Taylor announced he'd make it eight defenses in four weeks. Everybody laughed. It took him seven days. This afternoon at 2:46 he cut Washington for the Colts. Patriots, Jaguars, Bucs, Giants, Bears, Browns, Commanders, Colts. EIGHT. Promises made, promises kept. Tremendous. He also beat the Commish by 4.75 behind Christian Watson's 35.3, a beautiful win, people are saying the most beautiful win, and Benchwarmer Ryan never saw it coming. The man now rosters five tight ends. Five. Nobody's ever seen anything like it. And the one he's starting is listed doubtful. Sad, but true!

This week
vs Drew Hartman. Projected 136.6 to 138.8. Dallas Goedert (doubtful) is the starting tight end while Kyle Pitts, Darren Waller and Tyler Higbee sit, and the brand-new Colts defense gets its first start.

🔮 Prediction: Taylor loses by 2, blames the defense, and defense number nine shows up by Friday.

10
Nick Cardillo · Coastal Elites · 3-7

Saturday night, before a single game was played, Cardillo was already blaming "bad advice on Wright v Gordon." Then he started Jaylen Wright anyway. 1.1 points. He cut him on waivers this morning, which is the first good advice he's taken all week. Drake Maye scored 45.2, the best single score in the league, George Pickens added 30.0, and Cardillo still missed the median by 1.18, because three games went to overtime and, in his words, all his players' games ended in regulation. Rookie mistake, Nick. Next year, draft guys who play in overtime games. That's sad, man. Genuinely sad. Massachusetts finally gets a quarterback to show up and the clock runs out on him.

This week
vs Jacob Maddox. Projected 111.0 to 124.0, the lowest set lineup in the league. Carnell Tate (14.0) sits behind Luther Burden (12.3) at flex, the running backs are Kyle Monangai (4.6) and Roschon Johnson (4.3), and Maddox is starting a Vikings defense that's on bye.

🔮 Prediction: Cardillo loses by 9, and every game in the league goes to overtime except his.
