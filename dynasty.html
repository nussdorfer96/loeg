How it's calculated
  • Overall = 50% Win-Now + 50% Dynasty, scored against the other teams.
  • Win-Now = 50% results (record incl. the median game, points for, all-play) + 50% best possible lineup from Sleeper projections (rest of the regular season and this week), in our scoring.
  • Dynasty = total trade value of every rostered player plus 2027–2028 rookie picks, from FantasyCalc (superflex, 10 teams, PPR=1).
  • Status: Contender = top 4 in Win-Now; Rebuild = bottom 3 in Win-Now and outside the playoff line; everyone else Middle.

1

What is the deal with Maddox? He's 13-2-1, best record in the league, and I went through his transaction history to see the genius at work. Four real moves all season. Four! The rest is the Commish adding Shedeur Sanders to his roster and Maddox deleting him. Six times! Once in 12 minutes! That's not a GM, that's a spam filter. The man hasn't touched his roster since October 24. He's not building a dynasty, he's ignoring an inbox. And the whole league is in the chat begging "someone for the love of God beat Jacob." Beat him? You can't even get him to log in.

This week
vs Ryan Nussdorfer: 13-2-1 against 7-9, first against seventh. The whole league has a rooting interest Sunday, and for once it's THE Dictator. Fuck you Maddox.

🔮 Prediction: Maddox wins by 12 without logging in.

Second in the league in points. Seven and nine. THE Dictator has outscored everybody but Maddox and still has a losing record, which takes a special kind of leadership. He's left 171.8 points on his own bench this year. Thirty-two waiver and free-agent moves, and that's what he built: a bench. One win snaps a four-game skid and he's "so fucking back." Back to what? Seventh? And the 194 came against Brad. Congratulations. You beat the guy everybody beats. Engrave that one yourself.

This week
