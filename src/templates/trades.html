Trade Report Cards

Every trade graded by what each side got from the players it acquired, counting only points scored in that manager's starting lineup for the rest of that season (from the weekly box scores). Grades come from each side's share of the total: A+ at 75% or more, F under 35%. A trade decided by less than 5 points is a push.
