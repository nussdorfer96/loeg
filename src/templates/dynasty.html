How it's calculated
  • Overall = {{ (W.overall.win_now*100)|round|int }}% Win-Now + {{ (W.overall.dynasty*100)|round|int }}% Dynasty, scored against the other teams.
  • Win-Now = {{ (W.win_now.actual*100)|round|int }}% results (record incl. the median game, points for, all-play) + {{ (W.win_now.projected*100)|round|int }}% best possible lineup from Sleeper projections (rest of the regular season and this week), in our scoring.
