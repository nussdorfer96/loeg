The Season Story

The champion

Brian Mulvihill captured the Belt in 2024 with Wubba Lubba Dub Dubs, going 10-4 in the regular season as the #2 seed and pinning Nicholas Kasik (Torts Illustrated) in the title match. Ryan Nussdorfer entered the playoffs as the reigning Belt holder.

The title run

The title run: week 16: 128.12-109.48 over Kyle Llano; week 17: 150.06-149.98 over Nicholas Kasik.

Regular season

The regular season belonged to Ryan Nussdorfer (THE Commish), the #1 seed at 11-3, but the run ended in week 16 with a 135.22-175.88 loss to Nicholas Kasik.

Stars & the wire

Carrying the load: Lamar Jackson (QB) was the season's MVP for Kyle Llano. 407.4 fantasy points scored in his team's starting lineup for Gronk Nation.

Wire hero: Brian Mulvihill found Baker Mayfield (QB). Added in week 3 (waiver), then put up 286.76 starting points for Wubba Lubba Dub Dubs.

Draft room

Draft room verdict: Jerry Jeudy (WR) was the steal of the draft for Ryan Nussdorfer (Pick 15.02 (#142 overall) → finished WR11 with 228.6 points). On the other end, Andrew Saur spent a top pick on Christian McCaffrey (RB), who returned only 47.8 points (RB67).

Luck

Schedule victim of the year: Andrew Saur. Went 3-11 but an all-play record of 61-65 says 6.78 wins were deserved. 1,799.08 points against.

And the horseshoe award goes to Ryan Nussdorfer: Went 11-3 on an all-play pace of 8.33 wins (+2.67). Only 1,483.04 points against.

The basement

Bringing up the rear: Taylor Alvarado (East Lansing Nature Boy) finished last in the final standings at 4-10.

2024, the title game. Nicholas Kasik led with 2:58 left on Monday night. Then Jauan Jennings went to work in garbage time of a game the 49ers were losing by 12, and Brian Mulvihill won the championship by 0.08.

i How to read this
  • All-play record: your score that week against every other team, not just your opponent. In a 10-team league that's 9 games a week. It's the fairest measure of how good your team really was.
  • Expected wins: your all-play win % × the games you actually played. It's what a perfectly fair schedule would have given you.
  • Actual vs expected: the wins you got against the schedule you drew.
  • Luck (+/-): actual minus expected. +2 means the schedule handed you about two extra wins. -2 means it stole about two. Near 0 means your record was fair.

Real example: in 2024, Ryan Nussdorfer went 11-3, but his all-play record was 75-51 (0.595). That's worth 8.33 wins over 14 games, so he was +2.67 lucky. The flip side: Andrew Saur in 2024 went 3-11 with a 0.484 all-play, -3.78.
