The Season Story

The champion

Ryan Nussdorfer took the Belt straight off the waist of the reigning champ, Brian Mulvihill, in 2023 with THE Commish, going 8-6 in the regular season as the #2 seed and pinning Brian Mulvihill (Wubba Lubba Dub Dubs) in the title match.

The title run

The title run: week 16: 122.3-116.54 over Jacob Seeterlin; week 17: 166.28-134.92 over Brian Mulvihill.

Regular season

The regular season belonged to Bradley Kochheiser (Brad's Bad Boys), the #1 seed at 11-3, but the run ended in week 16 with a 127.68-135.8 loss to Brian Mulvihill.

Stars & the wire

Carrying the load: Christian McCaffrey (RB) was the season's MVP for Brian Mulvihill. 391.3 fantasy points scored in his team's starting lineup for Wubba Lubba Dub Dubs.

Wire hero: Bradley Kochheiser found Kyren Williams (RB). Added in week 2 (waiver), then put up 209.6 starting points for Brad's Bad Boys.

Draft room

Draft room verdict: Nico Collins (WR) was the steal of the draft for Bradley Kochheiser (Pick 13.07 (#127 overall) → finished WR18 with 225.9 points). On the other end, Parker Williams spent a top pick on Nick Chubb (RB), who returned only 23.1 points (RB88).

Luck

And the horseshoe award goes to Taylor Alvarado: Went 9-5 on an all-play pace of 6.78 wins (+2.22). Only 1,624.92 points against.

The basement

Bringing up the rear: Andrew Saur (Soft Sewell) finished last in the final standings at 5-9.

i How to read this
  • All-play record: your score that week against every other team, not just your opponent. In a 10-team league that's 9 games a week. It's the fairest measure of how good your team really was.
  • Expected wins: your all-play win % × the games you actually played. It's what a perfectly fair schedule would have given you.
  • Actual vs expected: the wins you got against the schedule you drew.
  • Luck (+/-): actual minus expected. +2 means the schedule handed you about two extra wins. -2 means it stole about two. Near 0 means your record was fair.

Real example: in 2024, Ryan Nussdorfer went 11-3, but his all-play record was 75-51 (0.595). That's worth 8.33 wins over 14 games, so he was +2.67 lucky. The flip side: Andrew Saur in 2024 went 3-11 with a 0.484 all-play, -3.78.
