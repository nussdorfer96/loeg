The Season Story

The champion

Jacob Maddox won the 2020 title with Why So Serious, going 8-5 in the regular season as the #3 seed and beating Ryan Nussdorfer (Password Is Taco) in the final. The Belt didn't exist until 2021, so there was no Belt to hand him. The title is his outright.

The title run

The title run: week 14: 117.52-106.82 over Brian Mulvihill; week 15: 151.46-96.82 over Drew Hartman; week 16: 136.42-115.58 over Ryan Nussdorfer.

Title-run trade (week 3): Jacob Maddox acquired Leonard Fournette, Julian Edelman from Ryan Nussdorfer for Todd Gurley II, Keenan Allen. Yes, the same manager they beat in the final.

Title-run trade (week 6): Jacob Maddox acquired Kenyan Drake, David Montgomery from Ryan Nussdorfer for CeeDee Lamb, Jerick McKinnon, Michael Thomas. Yes, the same manager they beat in the final.

Regular season

The regular season belonged to Ryan Nussdorfer (Password Is Taco), the #1 seed at 9-4, but the run ended in week 16 with a 115.58-136.42 loss to Jacob Maddox.

Brian Mulvihill led the league in points (1,668.44) and finished #6.

Stars & the wire

Carrying the load: Alvin Kamara (RB) was the season's MVP for Parker Williams. 377.8 fantasy points scored in his team's starting lineup for Deez Nutz.

Wire hero: Jacob Maddox found Justin Herbert (QB). Added in week 5 (free agent), then put up 239.92 starting points for Why So Serious.

Draft room

Draft room verdict: Ryan Tannehill (QB) was the steal of the draft for Brian Mulvihill (Pick 16.06 (#156 overall) → finished QB7 with 315.92 points). On the other end, Taylor Alvarado spent a top pick on Saquon Barkley (RB), who returned only 15.4 points (RB115).

Luck

Schedule victim of the year: Brian Mulvihill & Jonah Hohner. Went 6-7 but an all-play record of 70-47 says 7.78 wins were deserved. 1,613.06 points against. Tied at exactly the same mark with Jonah Hohner.

And the horseshoe award goes to Drew Hartman: Went 8-5 on an all-play pace of 5.67 wins (+2.33). Only 1,488.6 points against.

The basement

Bringing up the rear: Jonah Hohner (Okemos Nussdwarfers) finished last in the final standings at 4-9.

Trades

Trade log: 3 trades recorded in the weekly archive (see the transactions section below).

i How to read this
  • All-play record: your score that week against every other team, not just your opponent. In a 10-team league that's 9 games a week. It's the fairest measure of how good your team really was.
  • Expected wins: your all-play win % × the games you actually played. It's what a perfectly fair schedule would have given you.
  • Actual vs expected: the wins you got against the schedule you drew.
  • Luck (+/-): actual minus expected. +2 means the schedule handed you about two extra wins. -2 means it stole about two. Near 0 means your record was fair.

Real example: in 2024, Ryan Nussdorfer went 11-3, but his all-play record was 75-51 (0.595). That's worth 8.33 wins over 14 games, so he was +2.67 lucky. The flip side: Andrew Saur in 2024 went 3-11 with a 0.484 all-play, -3.78.
