The Season Story

The champion

Bradley Kochheiser captured the Belt in 2021 with Nussy's Lil Chubb, going 10-4 in the regular season as the #1 seed and pinning Ryan Nussdorfer (THE Commish) in the title match.

The title run

The title run: week 16: 122.28-100.54 over Drew Hartman; week 17: 157.36-143.44 over Ryan Nussdorfer.

Stars & the wire

Carrying the load: Cooper Kupp (WR) was the season's MVP for Parker Williams. 412.9 fantasy points scored in his team's starting lineup for Tyreek Hill's Daycare.

Wire hero: Ryan Nussdorfer found Diontae Johnson (WR). Added in week 4 (free agent), then put up 228.2 starting points for THE Commish.

Draft room

Draft room verdict: Jaylen Waddle (WR) was the steal of the draft for Brian Mulvihill (Pick 13.08 (#128 overall) → finished WR14 with 232.1 points (15.5 PPG over 15 games)). On the other end, Ryan Nussdorfer spent a top pick on Calvin Ridley (WR), who returned only 71.1 points (WR95, 14.2 PPG over 5 games).

Luck

Schedule victim of the year: Parker Williams. Went 5-9 but an all-play record of 62-64 says 6.89 wins were deserved. 1,771.84 points against.

And the horseshoe award goes to Jacob Maddox: Went 9-5 on an all-play pace of 6.67 wins (+2.33). Only 1,631.12 points against.

The basement

Bringing up the rear: Patrick Fitzgerald (Maddox Chase Young Kids) finished last in the final standings at 2-12.

Trades

Trade log: 1 trade recorded in the weekly archive (see the transactions section below).

i How to read this
  • All-play record: your score that week against every other team, not just your opponent. In a 10-team league that's 9 games a week. It's the fairest measure of how good your team really was.
  • Expected wins: your all-play win % × the games you actually played. It's what a perfectly fair schedule would have given you.
  • Actual vs expected: the wins you got against the schedule you drew.
  • Luck (+/-): actual minus expected. +2 means the schedule handed you about two extra wins. -2 means it stole about two. Near 0 means your record was fair.

Real example: in 2024, Ryan Nussdorfer went 11-3, but his all-play record was 75-51 (0.595). That's worth 8.33 wins over 14 games, so he was +2.67 lucky. The flip side: Andrew Saur in 2024 went 3-11 with a 0.484 all-play, -3.78.
