📜 The Story of the League

The ESPN redraft years

The League of Extraordinary Gentlemen kicked off on ESPN in 2020: ten teams, PPR scoring, and a fresh redraft every August. In 2021 the league introduced its WWE-style championship Belt. Over 6 seasons (2020-2025) 5 different managers won the title, and 17 different people ran a team.

2020: Jacob Maddox (Why So Serious) won it all as the #3 seed after an 8-5 regular season, beating Ryan Nussdorfer in the final. The Belt didn't exist yet; the title is his outright.

2021: Bradley Kochheiser (Nussy's Lil Chubb) won the Belt as the #1 seed after a 10-4 regular season, pinning Ryan Nussdorfer in the title match. First Belt holder in league history.

2022: Brian Mulvihill (Wubba Lubba Dub Dubs) won the Belt as the #2 seed after a 9-5 regular season, pinning Matthew Vinson in the title match.

2023: Ryan Nussdorfer (THE Commish) won the Belt as the #2 seed after an 8-6 regular season, pinning Brian Mulvihill in the title match and taking it from the reigning champ himself.

2024: Brian Mulvihill (Wubba Lubba Dub Dubs) won the Belt as the #2 seed after a 10-4 regular season, pinning Nicholas Kasik in the title match.

2025: Nick Cardillo (Wet Reckless) won the Belt as the #1 seed after a 9-5 regular season, pinning Parker Williams in the title match.

Brian Mulvihill is the league's only multi-time champion and two-time Belt holder (2022, 2024).

Moments that live forever

The single-game scoring record belongs to Taylor Alvarado: 189.25 points in week 8 of 2025 (East Lansing Nature Boy).

The most lopsided result: Drew Hartman over Jacob Maddox by 119.75 (129.15-9.4) in 2025, week 14.

The closest: Brian Mulvihill edged Nicholas Kasik 150.06-149.98 in 2024, week 17, a margin of 0.08.

Comings and goings

The iron men who played every ESPN season: Jacob Maddox, Ryan Nussdorfer, Taylor Alvarado, Drew Hartman, Brian Mulvihill, Parker Williams. 8 former managers have passed through: Eli Werner, Alec Adgate, Parker Williams, Jonah Hohner, Jacob Seeterlin, Andrew Saur, Patrick Fitzgerald, Matthew Vinson. Every stat stays with the person who earned it, so their numbers live on their own pages, and anyone who took over a team slot starts from zero.

2026: the dynasty era begins

For 2026 the league packed up and moved to Sleeper as a dynasty league: a 21-round startup draft, superflex lineups, a weekly game against the league median, 3 taxi slots, and tradeable future picks. New blood arrived too: Mike Hemstreet joined the league. Through 3 weeks, HulkSmash (Jacob Maddox) leads the way.
