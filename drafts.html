Draft Trends

How the league drafts, from every ESPN draft from 2020 to 2025. A hit is a pick in rounds 1-6 who finished as a starter at his position (top 12 QB/TE, top 24 RB, top 30 WR). A bust is a round 1-6 pick who finished worse than twice that. Value vs finish compares where a player was drafted with where he ranked by end-of-season points above replacement. Plus means the manager got more than he paid for.

ADP isn't used. ESPN only reports an average draft position for players still rostered at season's end, and it's an end-of-season snapshot (for example, 2025 ADPs show the 170 placeholder for first-rounders). Reaches are measured against how the player finished instead.
